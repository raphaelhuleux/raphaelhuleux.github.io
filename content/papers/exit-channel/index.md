##### Abstract

We study why monetary tightening can push small but very productive and fast-growing firms out of the market. These firms are highly leveraged during their growth period and are thus highly sensitive to changes in interest rates. Monetary tightening can therefore worsen the selection of firms, with long-term effects on growth and productivity.
